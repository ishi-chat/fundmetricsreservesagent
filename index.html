Fund Finance Agent

An AI agent for fund finance: reserves adequacy (follow-on capacity versus implied need, who gets crowded out) and performance metrics (DPI, RVPI, TVPI, XIRR, eight-line IC note). The model plans and explains; all arithmetic runs in deterministic calculators it calls as tools. Built by Ishita Chaturvedi, CA, CFA.

Illustrative portfolio with invented company names. Not any fund's data. Not investment advice. Methodology demo only. Source: github.com/b8hybz2vh6-ai/fundmetricsreservesagent
